# Chalkday — Brand identity

**Slug:** `chalkday` · **Tagline:** A weekly specials and promo calendar for independent restaurants and cafes.
**Status:** Live. Payments processed by Gumroad (Monthly or Quarterly).

## Positioning
Single-location restaurants, cafes, bars, and trucks that want a steady specials habit without a marketing agency.

**Promise (honest):** Menu-planning ideas only. No cover-count or sales guarantees.

## Personas
- **Owner-chef:** Great ideas, no time to plan them.
- **GM / marketer:** You need a calendar the kitchen agrees to.
- **Cafe owner:** Small menu, regulars who want something new.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Plan** — pain: Specials are decided at 4 pm. → plan: A 4-week rhythm decided in one sitting.
2. **Prep** — pain: Specials strain the line. → plan: Ideas sized to your prep capacity.
3. **Promote** — pain: Nobody knows the special exists. → plan: Board lines, posts, and a staff talk track.
4. **Review** — pain: No idea what worked. → plan: A 10-minute weekly debrief sheet.

## Voice & tone
Warm, brisk, kitchen-smart. Short like a pre-shift brief; a little chalkboard charm.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

- Display: **Caveat Brush**

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing
- **Monthly** — $29 / month. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
- **Quarterly** — $79 every 3 months. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.

## Do
- Talk like a kitchen: mise, pre-shift, 86’d.
- Remind owners to cost every special and follow health code.
- Show Monthly and Quarterly prices, plus the renewal sentence, before checkout.
- Save the customer’s email before sending them to Gumroad.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Make nutrition, diet, or health claims.
- Promise more covers or sales.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
